PyDebug Collaborator Program Rules

Effective: 9 November 2026. By applying or participating, you agree to these rules. This program is a marketing partnership, not employment, an agency, or a promise of guaranteed income.

  1. Eligibility and approval. You must use your own verified PyDebug account and provide accurate name, promotion profile and payout details. Applicants must be at least 18. Applying does not guarantee approval. PyDebug may approve, reject, pause or end participation for safety, quality, legal or business reasons.
  2. Coupon and attribution. PyDebug assigns your coupon code and decides its discount, plans, expiry and limits. A sale is attributed only when a customer uses your assigned coupon in an eligible PyDebug checkout and Razorpay payment is verified. A click, signup or an unused code alone does not earn commission; there is no cookie-only attribution in this version. If another coupon is applied, the checkout’s recorded code controls.
  3. Commission basis. Your admin dashboard displays your individual rate. Commission is calculated on the verified amount actually captured after discount, in INR, not the plan’s undiscounted list price. The percentage and partner attribution are snapshotted when the payment order is created; later rate or coupon changes apply only to new orders. No commission is due for failed/unverified payments, self-referrals, duplicate/fake accounts, bots, abuse, cancellations, refunds or chargebacks. A partial refund may result in a proportional adjustment.
  4. Pending period and weekly settlement. A verified sale remains pending for 7 days from its recorded payment grant to allow refund/chargeback checks. Eligible unpaid balance is prepared for payout on Sunday, India Standard Time (Asia/Kolkata), with no minimum payout threshold. Sales still inside the 7-day hold carry forward to a later Sunday. At present PyDebug processes transfers manually; a dashboard entry is not proof of transfer. A payout becomes “paid” only when PyDebug records the successful transfer reference.
  5. Payout details and taxes. You are responsible for keeping a correct UPI ID/bank payout destination and notifying PyDebug if it changes. Incorrect details or bank/network delays may defer settlement. You are responsible for your applicable tax reporting and invoices; PyDebug may withhold or request tax details where required by law. Do not send passwords, OTPs or card details to anyone.
  6. Promotion standards. Clearly disclose that you may earn commission (for example “affiliate/paid partnership” or #ad). Do not make false product, earning or discount claims; spam, impersonate PyDebug, buy fake traffic, use unauthorized brand assets, or promise a discount/benefit not displayed at checkout. Do not use your own code or encourage users to create duplicate accounts to earn commission.
  7. Review, corrections and disputes. PyDebug may review suspicious orders and correct demonstrable calculation or refund errors. Report a payout discrepancy within 14 days of the relevant dashboard entry, with the coupon/order reference (never share a customer’s password or payment credentials). Unpaid commissions under review may be held. Already-paid amounts are not silently clawed back; fraud or legal claims are handled separately.
  8. Program changes and ending participation. PyDebug may change future discounts, rates, eligible plans, or these rules with notice in the portal. Existing verified orders keep their snapshotted rate unless the order is refunded, charged back, invalid or unlawful. On suspension/termination, stop using PyDebug promotional material and codes; valid already-earned eligible balance remains subject to these rules and applicable law.

Questions or legal/tax concerns should be raised with PyDebug before promotion. These program rules should be reviewed by PyDebug’s Indian legal/tax advisor before public launch.
